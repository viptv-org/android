dts-tone.mka contains a synthetic three-second 440 Hz tone, six channels,
768 kbit/s DTS, 48 kHz, generated by:

ffmpeg -f lavfi -i sine=frequency=440:sample_rate=48000 -t 3 -ac 6
  -c:a dca -strict -2 -b:a 768k -metadata:s:a:0 language=eng dts-tone.mka

This owned fixture tests actual Media3 extraction, selection, software decoding
and audio-output clock advancement without a network or platform DTS decoder.
